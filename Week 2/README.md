# Week 2 — Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This week focused on analysing the cleaned Superstore sales dataset to understand overall business performance, sales trends, regional performance, and category performance.

The analysis was completed using Excel and data visualizations to identify important patterns and business insights.

## Objectives

* Calculate key business performance indicators (KPIs).
* Analyse sales performance over time.
* Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Compare sales and profitability across regions.
* Compare sales, profit, and quantity across product categories.
* Identify important business insights from the analysis.

## Sales Trend Analysis

The analysis examined sales performance from 2014 to 2017, as well as monthly sales patterns.

The dataset showed an overall increase in sales across the four-year period. November recorded the highest cumulative monthly sales, while February recorded the lowest cumulative monthly sales.

Monthly sales and profit trends were visualised to identify seasonal patterns and periods of stronger or weaker performance.

## Regional Analysis

The four regions were compared using:

* Sales
* Profit
* Orders
* Profit margin

The West region recorded the highest sales and also generated the highest total profit.

This comparison helped assess whether the region generating the most revenue was also contributing strongly to profitability.

## Category Analysis

The three product categories analysed were:

* Technology
* Furniture
* Office Supplies

Technology recorded the highest sales and highest total profit among the three categories.

Category performance was compared using sales, profit, and quantity sold.

## Key Business Insights

1. Total sales reached approximately $2.30 million across the dataset.
2. The business generated approximately $286,397 in total profit.
3. Sales increased overall between 2014 and 2017.
4. The West region was the strongest region in terms of both sales and profit.
5. Technology was the strongest-performing category by sales and profit.
6. November had the highest cumulative monthly sales, while February had the lowest.
